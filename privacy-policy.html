Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information. Last updated: September 2026.

Plain-English summary: we only collect what we need to reply to your enquiry and provide our bookkeeping service — your name, contact details, and anything you tell us in your message. We don't sell your data or run ad-tracking on this site, and you can ask us to access or delete your information at any time. The full details are below.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the Ledgerly Bookkeeping website (ledgerly.ie) and any related services, and describes how we collect, use, share, and protect personal information in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Irish Data Protection Act 2018.

Ledgerly Bookkeeping is the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. If you have any questions or requests relating to your data, you can reach us at hello@ledgerly.ie or (01) 555 0142.

By using this website or submitting information to us, you confirm that any personal information you provide is true, complete, and accurate.

2. Information we collect

We collect personal information in the following ways:

  • Contact form submissions: full name, business name, email address, phone number, the plan you're interested in, and any details you include in your message.
  • Direct contact: anything you share with us by phone or email when getting in touch.
  • Basic website usage data: if we use analytics tools, we may collect general technical information such as browser type, device type, and pages visited, to understand how the site is used.

We do not collect payment or banking details through this website, and we do not knowingly collect any special category (sensitive) personal data, such as health or financial background information, through the site itself.

3. How we use your information

We use the personal information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To deliver our services: responding to your enquiry, providing the free quote or consultation you requested, and carrying out bookkeeping work for clients
  • To communicate with you: answering questions, sending updates about your enquiry or engagement, and administrative messages
  • To run and improve our business: understanding how our website is used and improving our services
  • To meet our legal and professional obligations: including bookkeeping, tax, and record-keeping requirements we're subject to as a business

4. Legal basis for processing

Depending on the situation, we rely on one of the following legal bases under GDPR to process your personal information:

  • Consent — for example, when you voluntarily submit the contact form. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Performance of a contract — where processing is necessary to provide bookkeeping services once you become a client.
  • Legal obligation — where we must process or retain information to comply with Irish tax, company, or data protection law.
  • Legitimate interests — such as responding to enquiries, keeping our website secure, and running our business, balanced against your rights and interests.

5. How we share your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising. We may share it with:

  • Service providers who help us operate, such as our email provider, website host, and — for clients — cloud accounting software (Xero, QuickBooks, or Sage), each bound by their own confidentiality and data protection obligations
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, including Revenue or the Data Protection Commission, where we are required to disclose information by law
  • A new owner, in the unlikely event of a sale, merger, or transfer of the business, subject to the same protections set out in this policy

If you become a client, any bookkeeping and financial data you share with us as part of that engagement is handled separately and confidentially, under the terms agreed when we begin working together — see our Terms of Service.

6. Cookies and tracking technologies

This website may use basic cookies or similar technologies to help it function correctly and, where enabled, general analytics tools to understand visitor traffic (such as pages viewed and general location). We do not currently use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. You can control or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not function as intended if you do.

This site does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals, as there is no accepted industry standard for how to interpret them.

7. Data retention

We keep contact form enquiries only for as long as needed to respond to you, and delete or anonymise them after a reasonable period if no engagement follows. Where you become a client, financial records and related personal information are retained in line with our professional and statutory obligations — in Ireland, this generally means keeping business records for at least six years, in line with Revenue requirements. Once information is no longer needed for these purposes, we securely delete or anonymise it.

8. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or misuse, including access controls and secure, reputable software providers. No method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we work to protect your information to a high standard.

9. Your privacy rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Ask us to delete your information ("right to be forgotten"), subject to any legal retention obligations
  • Restrict or object to how we process your information
  • Request a copy of your information in a portable, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@ledgerly.ie. We will respond within the timeframe required by law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission (DPC) at www.dataprotection.ie if you believe your data protection rights have been breached.

10. Children's privacy

Our website and services are directed at business owners and are not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we will delete any such information if we become aware it has been collected.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date, so please check back periodically.
