Who We Help

Bookkeeping built around how your business actually works.

Every industry keeps its books a little differently. Here's how Ledgerly fits the businesses we work with most.

🧑‍💻

Sole Traders & Freelancers

Invoicing clients, tracking expenses, and staying ready for your Form 11 self-assessment — without it eating into billable time. We keep your income and expenses reconciled monthly, so tax season is a formality, not a scramble.

🔧

Tradespeople

Electricians, plumbers, builders, and tilers deal with supplier invoices, van and tool expenses, and often subcontractors paid under RCT. We keep it all categorised correctly so nothing gets missed at year-end.

🛒

E-Commerce & Online Sellers

Multiple payment platforms, marketplace fees, and returns can make reconciliation messy fast. We untangle Stripe, PayPal, and marketplace payouts into clean, accurate books — including VAT on cross-border sales.

☕

Retail & Hospitality

Cafes, shops, and salons deal with high transaction volumes, till reconciliations, and staff payroll. We handle the daily detail so you can focus on the counter, not the spreadsheet.

💼

Consultants & Professional Services

Retainers, mixed income streams, and business expenses that blur with personal ones. We keep your books clean and your numbers clear, so you always know what's actually left after tax.

🚀

Small Limited Companies & Startups

Early-stage companies need clean books from day one — for investors, for your accountant, and for your own sanity. We set you up properly on Xero, QuickBooks, or Sage and keep things audit-ready as you grow.

Don't See Your Business Above?

If it involves invoices, receipts, and a bank account — we can help.

These are just the industries we work with most often. Bookkeeping fundamentals are the same across almost any small business, and we tailor our approach to how yours actually runs.
