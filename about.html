About Ledgerly

Bookkeeping that takes the stress off your shoulders.

Ledgerly is a bookkeeping practice built around one goal: making your books stress-free, and making sure you always understand exactly where your business stands financially.

Why Ledgerly Exists

Small business bookkeeping shouldn't be stressful or expensive.

Too many small business owners either put off their books until it's a last-minute scramble, or pay big-firm prices for basic bookkeeping they could get more affordably elsewhere. Ledgerly was built to fix both problems at once.

We handle the day-to-day bookkeeping — reconciliations, records, reporting — so you're never scrambling to find a receipt or guessing where your money went. And we explain everything in plain English, so you're not just handed a spreadsheet, you actually understand what it means for your business.

The result is bookkeeping that's dependable, genuinely affordable, and takes the stress out of your finances for good.

Our Mission

Stress-free bookkeeping. Finances you understand.

We believe small business owners deserve two things from their bookkeeper: total peace of mind, and a clear, honest picture of how their business is really doing.

Important: we're not accountants

Ledgerly supports your accountant — it doesn't replace one. We handle bookkeeping, reconciliations, and calculations up to trial balance, including preparing VAT and year-end figures. We don't file VAT returns or prepare and file statutory year-end accounts; that final step still needs a qualified accountant. Our goal is to take the time-consuming groundwork off their desk, so your accountant's job is quicker and your overall bill is lower.
