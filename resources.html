The Monthly Bookkeeping Checklist

Run through this once a month and you'll never face a year-end scramble. It's the same rhythm we use for every client we work with.

  • Reconcile your bank account against your bookkeeping records
  • File or photograph every receipt from the month — don't let paper pile up
  • Confirm every transaction is categorised correctly (nothing left as "uncategorised")
  • Check for any subscriptions still being billed that should've been cancelled
  • Review outstanding invoices — chase anything more than 30 days overdue
  • Review upcoming bills and payments due in the next few weeks
  • Check your business and personal spending haven't been mixed
  • Look at your simple cash position — money in, money out, what's left
  • Note anything unusual to ask your bookkeeper or accountant about
  • Back up or export your records if you're not on cloud software

Irish VAT Deadline Calendar

Most small businesses file VAT bi-monthly. Here are the standard filing periods and due dates for VAT3 returns filed through ROS (Revenue Online Service).

  • Jan – Feb → due 23rd March
  • Mar – Apr → due 23rd May
  • May – Jun → due 23rd July
  • Jul – Aug → due 23rd September
  • Sep – Oct → due 23rd November
  • Nov – Dec → due 23rd January

This is general guidance, not tax advice. Some businesses file monthly, annually, or on a different schedule depending on their VAT registration — and exact deadlines can shift slightly around weekends and bank holidays. Always confirm your specific dates on revenue.ie or with your accountant.
