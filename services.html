What We Do

Bookkeeping services built for small business budgets.

Fixed monthly pricing, no hidden fees, and careful, dependable bookkeeping behind every account we manage. Pick a plan below, or get in touch for something custom.

ℹ️

Important: we're not accountants

Ledgerly is a bookkeeping support service, not an accountancy practice. We handle day-to-day bookkeeping, reconciliations, and calculations up to trial balance — including preparing the figures behind your VAT and year-end. We do not file VAT returns or prepare and file statutory year-end financial statements — those need to be reviewed, signed off, and filed by a qualified accountant. Ledgerly is designed to work alongside your accountant (or help you find one), taking the heavy lifting off their plate so your overall accounting costs come down.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How can your prices be so much lower than a traditional accounting firm?

We specialise exclusively in bookkeeping for small businesses, run lean, and use efficient cloud software — so we pass the savings straight to you, without cutting corners on accuracy.

What software do you work with?

We work with Xero, QuickBooks Online, and Sage. If you're not currently using accounting software, we'll help you get set up at no extra cost.

Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime?

No long-term contracts. All plans are month-to-month, and you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel whenever your business needs change.

Do you work with businesses outside Dublin?

Yes — everything is handled remotely and securely, so we work with small businesses across Ireland.

What if I'm behind on my bookkeeping already?

That's exactly what our Catch-Up & Cleanup service is for. We'll assess the backlog, quote a fair one-off fee, and get you current before moving you onto a monthly plan.

Are you an accountant?

No — Ledgerly is a bookkeeping support service, not an accountancy practice. We handle reconciliations, records, and calculations up to trial balance, including VAT and year-end prep, but we don't file VAT returns or prepare and file statutory year-end accounts. We work alongside your accountant to save them time — and save you money — rather than replacing them.

Do I still need an accountant if I use Ledgerly?

Yes. Ledgerly handles the ongoing bookkeeping so that when VAT or year-end comes around, your accountant has clean, organised figures ready to review, sign off, and file — which usually means a faster job and a smaller accountant's bill.
